# How to update your website 👋

Hi! This is your website. You update it by **just talking to Claude Code** — no
web builder, no code, no "git." This page explains the whole thing in plain
language. It's short. You've got this.

## The one idea to remember: Preview vs Live

- **Preview** = a private draft only *you* can see, so you can check a change
  before anyone else does.
- **Live** = your real website that the public sees, at
  thebarnfitnesscollective.com.

Nothing you do is public until you say **"publish it."**

## How a change works (start to finish)

1. **Tell Claude what you want**, in normal words. For example:
   - "Change the Unlimited membership to $209 a month."
   - "Add a new coach named Sam — here's their photo and a short bio."
   - "Fix the typo in the FAQ about parking."
   - "Add a new class called Sunrise Strength on Sundays."

2. **Claude makes the change and shows you a Preview.** It'll say something like
   *"Here's a preview — open http://localhost:4321"* (or just show you a picture
   of it right in the chat). Look it over.

3. **You say yes or ask for tweaks.** "A bit bigger," "make it coral," "never
   mind, put it back" — just keep talking. It updates instantly.

4. **When you're happy, say "publish it."** Claude puts it Live. About 30 seconds
   later it's on the real website. Claude will confirm when it's up.

That's the whole loop: **describe → preview → publish.**

## If something looks wrong after publishing

Just say **"undo that"** or **"put the site back to how it was."** Claude can roll
it back to the previous version in seconds. You can't permanently break anything
— every version is saved.

## Handy things to say

| You want to… | Just say… |
| --- | --- |
| See a change before it's public | (Claude previews automatically — nothing to do) |
| Check it on your phone or send it to Wyatt/Zoe first | "Give me a preview link I can open on my phone" |
| Make it public | "Publish it" / "Make it live" |
| Undo a change | "Undo that" / "Roll back the site" |
| Change a price, class, coach, photo, FAQ | Just describe it plainly |

## Good to know

- **Previewing is free and private.** Play around as much as you want — nothing
  goes public until you say so.
- **You never touch code or "git."** Claude handles all of that behind the scenes.
- **Photos are easy now.** Just give Claude the image (any size — it'll size it
  for the web automatically) and say where it goes.
- If Claude ever asks you to click something in **Netlify** (the host) or
  **GitHub**, that's normal for the rare setup/account step — it'll walk you
  through exactly what to click.

Questions? Ask Wyatt, or just ask Claude "how do I…" — it knows this site.
